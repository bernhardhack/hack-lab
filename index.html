AI Literacy Track

Four modules, in order. Everything here runs on an iPad — no printer, no robot, no waiting on anyone. That is deliberate: this track keeps moving on the weeks when the hardware doesn't.

MODULE 01 iPad only

Teach the Machine

Train an image classifier in the browser on something from the game — a hand sign, a token, a card. Then collect a second set of photos in different light and watch it fall apart.

The point: a model is only as good as what it was shown.

MODULE 02 Needs Module 01

Break It

Your job is to fool the classifier you just built. Wrong angles, shadows, a drawing of the thing instead of the thing. Every successful attack goes in the log.

The point: confident and correct are not the same thing.

Adversarial Mission log
MODULE 03 Needs Dad's uplink

The Oracle

A character in the game, run by a language model, where the system prompt is the character sheet. Emma writes the voice and what it must never reveal. Jacob writes the world rules it has to respect.

The point: you shape a model by what you tell it it is.

MODULE 04 Needs SPIKE

Ground Truth

Before the rover drives, write down what you think each sensor will report. Then drive it and compare. The gap between the prediction and the log is the whole mission.

The point: sensors don't perceive. They measure.
